# Dashboard

A dashboard in the sidebar (*HomeBatteryAssist*), in tabs. The chosen tab is remembered per browser. The title bar shows the installed version. Times and day boundaries are those of Home Assistant's time zone (as in the *Why* texts), also when the browser is in another one.

- **Overview**: the active planner, consumption forecaster, tariff, PV correction and interceptor with what it holds back or limits now (with a warning on a fallback); the energy flows now (and which grid meter is used), a price tile with the market, import and export price now and from the next price change; the grid connection per phase, centred at zero (export to the left, import to the right) with a total bar on 3 phases; what phase protection and peak shaving do right now; an **energy flow diagram** (solar, grid, battery, house, excluded devices such as an EV charger, and the heating/cooling zones as part of the house) with the power now (moving dots along the flows) and per node a small table of today so far and the whole day as expected (kWh: measured so far plus the forecast and the battery plan until midnight; excluded devices are not forecast), and the totals of today below; with ◀ ▶ any earlier day of the history (up to 28 days): the energy of that day per part and per flow (kWh, lines as thick as the energy along them); the latest decisions (*Why*, also at the bottom of *Forecast*).
- **Forecast**: energy flows and expected consumption with the prices on a second axis (published solid, forecast dashed), with background bands for what the batteries did (measured) and are planned to do: charging with solar (green), charging from the grid (purple), discharging (blue), and solar without battery activity (faint yellow), and the batteries' total fill as a grey area behind it (measured, then planned); 36 hours per screen width, with a horizontal scrollbar for longer ranges; by default over the whole forecast — or today / today + tomorrow, and browsable back into the history; the batteries' state of charge (measured and planned); the last 7 days; the average day with a line per day of the week (Mon–Sun) and the average of all days dashed, browsable per week.
  - **Planning**: how far ahead can be planned and what limits it, with a bar per forecast showing how far it reaches: consumption, prices, PV and (with a weather source) the outdoor temperature.
  - **Temperature** (on *Forecast*, with a weather source): the forecast outdoor temperature, the learned room temperature and the heating/cooling power to expect, with the expected kWh per day; until 7 days are learned only the outdoor temperature.
- **Return**: the expected and realised return of each mode compared with no battery and with each other; the plan compared with what really happened, per day.
- **Batteries**: each battery with its state of charge, power, time to full/empty (now, by the plan and at zero on the meter), the details its driver adds (temperature, voltages, cells, cycles), the energy charged and discharged today and the [measured efficiency](measuring.md#today-and-measured-efficiency).
- **Accuracy**: how well the [plan was followed](planning.md#plan-check) (last 7 days, virtual and real batteries); the accuracy of the consumption (and its heating/cooling part), PV and price forecasts per lead time, with their corrections; the consumption forecasters compared; and the PV corrections compared (with the uncorrected forecast for reference).

The plan mode can be chosen right on the dashboard.
