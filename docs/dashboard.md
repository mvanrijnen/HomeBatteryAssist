# Dashboard

A dashboard in the sidebar (*HomeBatteryAssist*), in tabs. The chosen tab is remembered per browser. The title bar shows the installed version. Times and day boundaries are those of Home Assistant's time zone (as in the *Why* texts), also when the browser is in another one.

The top bar has two buttons next to *Recalculate*:

- **Charge the car**: when to switch the car's charging on for the lowest expected cost. Fill in the energy to charge (kWh, from the grid connection), the charging power (kW) and the period (*not before* and *ready by*). The answer is one block, switched on once and off once: when, the expected cost and the average price per kWh, how much of it comes from the expected solar surplus (after the batteries; that costs only what exporting it would have earned), and what starting at once would cost. It uses the prices and solar forecast of the selected plan, so the end can lie no further ahead than the prices reach. Advice only: nothing is switched (controlling the charger is for later). The energy and power are remembered in the browser.
- **Occasional consumption**: see [Forecasting](forecasting.md#consumption).

- **Overview**: the active planner, consumption forecaster, tariff, PV correction and interceptor with what it holds back or limits now (with a warning on a fallback); **the plan at a glance** (now with the price thresholds, next, the plan in one line, the sun, the cheapest price; see [Planning](planning.md#explanation)); the energy flows now (and which grid meter is used), a price tile with the market, import and export price now and from the next price change; the grid connection per phase, centred at zero (export to the left, import to the right) with a total bar on 3 phases; what phase protection and peak shaving do right now; an **energy flow diagram** (solar, grid, battery, house, excluded devices such as an EV charger, and the heating/cooling zones as part of the house) with the power now (moving dots along the flows) and per node a small table of today so far and the whole day as expected (kWh: measured so far plus the forecast and the battery plan until midnight; excluded devices are not forecast), and the totals of today below; with ◀ ▶ any earlier day of the history (up to 28 days): the energy of that day per part and per flow (kWh, lines as thick as the energy along them); the latest decisions (*Why*, also at the bottom of *Forecast*).
- **Forecast**: energy flows and expected consumption with the prices on a second axis (published solid, forecast dashed), with background bands for what the batteries did (measured) and are planned to do: charging with solar (green), charging from the grid (purple), discharging (blue), and solar without battery activity (faint yellow), and the batteries' total fill as a grey area behind it (measured, then planned); 36 hours per screen width, with a horizontal scrollbar for longer ranges; by default over the whole forecast — or today / today + tomorrow, and browsable back into the history; the batteries' state of charge (measured and planned); the last 7 days; the average day with a line per day of the week (Mon–Sun) and the average of all days dashed, browsable per week, with under it a table of the kWh per day of the week (and of the week shown next to all history).
  - **Planning**: how far ahead can be planned and what limits it, with a bar per forecast showing how far it reaches: consumption, prices, PV and (with a weather source) the outdoor temperature.
  - **Temperature** (on *Forecast*, with a weather source): the forecast outdoor temperature, the learned room temperature and the heating/cooling power to expect, with the expected kWh per day; until 7 days are learned only the outdoor temperature.
- **Return**: the expected and realised return of each mode compared with no battery and with each other; the plan compared with what really happened, per day.
- **Batteries**: each battery with its state of charge, power, time to full/empty (now, by the plan and at zero on the meter), the details its driver adds (temperature, voltages, cells, cycles), the energy charged and discharged today and the [measured efficiency](measuring.md#today-and-measured-efficiency).
- **Accuracy**: how well the [plan was followed](planning.md#plan-check) (last 7 days, virtual and real batteries); the accuracy of the consumption (and its heating/cooling part), PV and price forecasts per lead time, with their corrections; the consumption forecasters compared; and the PV corrections compared (with the uncorrected forecast for reference).

The plan mode can be chosen right on the dashboard.
