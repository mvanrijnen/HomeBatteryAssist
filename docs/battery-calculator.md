# Battery calculator

!!! warning "From 1 January 2027"
    For a **dynamic contract** (a price per hour or quarter) in the Netherlands, **without net metering** (*salderen*), which ends on 1 January 2027. With net metering (2026) the outcomes are not right.

Fill in your own prices, supplier fees and battery; the outcomes follow at once. All amounts are per kWh. Nothing is sent anywhere: the calculation runs in your browser.

Three cases:

- **Grid charge → use yourself:** charge when the price is low, use the energy yourself when it is high. Energy tax and the purchase fee cancel out (you pay them either way); only the losses and wear count.
- **Grid charge → export:** charge from the grid and export later. Without net metering you pay VAT and energy tax on the purchase but only get the bare market price back, so it hardly ever pays.
- **Store solar → use yourself:** your solar surplus costs you only the export price you miss; it nearly always pays.

The wear is the purchase price ÷ cycles ÷ usable kWh ÷ 2 (every kWh goes in once and out once). Use a realistic number of cycles (about 3000), not the specification: at about 300 cycles a year a battery ages out before it cycles out.
